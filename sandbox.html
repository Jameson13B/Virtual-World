    ❔ How to play
    
      
    
        
    
          
    🛣️ Roads editor (1)
    
          
      
            
    • Click empty space: add point & connect to selected
      • 
            
    • Click a road: split it into a junction
      • 
            
    • Drag a point: move it
      • 
            
    • Right-click: deselect → delete point → delete road
      • 
            
    • O on a road: cycle one-way ↔ two-way
      • 
            
    • Delete: remove selected point
      • 
            
    • Esc: deselect
      • 
          
    
          
    🚦 Marking tools (2–8)
    
          
      
            
    • Hover a lane, click to place
      • 
            
    • Hold Shift to flip direction
      • 
            
    • Right-click a marking to remove it
      • 
          
    
        
    
        
    
          
    ▶️ Drive (P)
    
          
      
            
    • WASD / Arrows: drive · Shift: boost
      • 
            
    • R: respawn · C: re-center camera
      • 
            
    • Follow the blue GPS line to the 🎯 target
      • 
            
    • Stop inside a 🅿️ spot to park
      • 
          
    
          
    🧠 Train AI (T)
    
          
      
            
    • Cars with random brains drive from the start marking
      • 
            
    • The best brain is saved and mutated each generation
      • 
            
    • Use it in Drive mode with “Add AI driver”
      • 
          
    
          
    🎥 Camera
    
          
      
            
    • Wheel / pinch: zoom · Space+drag or middle-drag: pan
      • 
            
    • F: fit world · N: night · M: minimap
      • 
            
    • Click the minimap to jump
      • 
            
    • Ctrl/Cmd+Z undo · Ctrl/Cmd+S save
      •
